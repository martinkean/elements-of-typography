Bauhaus & Typography: A Modernist Marriage

Body

The Bauhaus, a groundbreaking German art school operating from 1919 to 1933, left an undeniable mark on the world of design. Its influence extends far beyond architecture and furniture, reaching deep into the realm of typography. The Bauhaus approach championed a clean, functional aesthetic that continues to define modern design principles.

One of the most significant contributions of the Bauhaus to typography was the popularization of sans-serif fonts. These typefaces, lacking the decorative flourishes found on traditional serif fonts, were seen as more legible and suited to the fast-paced world of modern communication.
